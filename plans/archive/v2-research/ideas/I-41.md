# I-41: Cross-session transcript search

Card from the [Loom v2 idea catalog](../IDEAS.md). **Cross-session transcript search** · verdict **adopt-light** · cost M · value 3/5 · proposed by 4 reports: [orca](../products/orca.md), [cmux](../products/cmux.md), [cursor](../products/cursor.md), [power-user-workflows](../products/power-user-workflows.md).

> Build the no-index, on-demand, budgeted transcript scan as a second stage of the existing sidebar search; skip the FTS5 index, the Cmd-K palette and in-panel Cmd+F for v2 unless a half-day measurement of the user's real corpus says the scan is too slow.

## What the user gets

'Which session did I solve X in / which touched EDDP-123' becomes a keystroke: content hits in the existing sidebar search (and a Cmd-K palette), ranked above loose title matches, showing the matching line and opening at the right place; Cmd+F inside a session's transcript panel.

## Mechanisms found in the research

Lightest, no index: search the most recent N sessions' JSONL on demand with a time and byte budget and a streaming matcher, cancel on typing (Cursor-style bounded variant). Heavier: incremental SQLite FTS5 index fed by the same FSEvents transcript reader as Usage, user and assistant text only (not tool output or scrollback), size-capped, rebuildable from transcripts so CLI format drift degrades coverage not correctness, operators agent:/repo:/before:, nothing running when the search UI is closed. Deletion: the permanent-delete flow must purge that session's index rows in the same transaction and report it.

## Debates and fact-check corrections

Index growth (Orca had unbounded-growth complaints) vs an on-demand scan; an index is a second copy of sensitive conversation text (needs an off switch and per-project exclusion). Transcript format is internal and unversioned; transcripts are subject to cleanupPeriodDays (default 30, configurable). power-user-workflows places it in the 'after measuring' tier.

## Design in Loom


FACTS (read in the repo): (1) Loom already walks, tails and parses transcripts for Usage: `UsageTranscriptWalker.candidates(profile:)` and `isTranscriptName` (skips `.orphaned-`/`.superseded-` variants and the `memory` and `tool-results` folders), `UsageTranscriptWatcher` (one FSEvents stream over `<profile>/projects`, no timer), `ClaudeUsageIndex` (disposable `usage-index.sqlite3`, per-file offset plus 64-byte guard hash, forget digests), and `SubagentTranscriptTail` and `SubagentTranscriptParser` (partial last line held back, 128 KiB line cap, text-only entries that never decode `toolUseResult`). Catalog search is `SessionCatalog.searchSessions`, built on `conditions(for:)` in `SessionCatalog+Paging.swift` (LIKE over `loom_fold(provider|title|project name)`), driven by `CatalogModel.setSearchText` (120 ms debounce, cancel-and-replace) and `CatalogModel+Search.reloadSearchResults` (generation guard). Deletion already has `ConversationForgetters`, called in the `verifying` step with Loom id plus all provider ids, idempotent, and a throw keeps the deletion open (`ConversationDeletion.swift`, registered for usage in `AppDelegate+Usage.swift:34`). The existing U0 evidence measured the usage cold scan at about 241 MiB/s on a 1 GiB synthetic corpus (4.2 s, +14 MB RSS, warm cache) in `plans/experiments/run1-U0-usage-index.json`; a no-decode memmem-style gate should be several times faster, a decode-and-write indexer about the same speed.

DESIGN (V1, no persistent state):
1. LoomCore `TranscriptSearch` (new file, no UI). Input: the query, a candidate list, a `SearchBudget`. Candidates come from the walker (path, session stem, size, mtime; one directory pass, milliseconds) joined to catalog rows by lowercase provider id. Join `sessions.provider_conversation_id` plus `provider_identity_changes` so a /clear chain maps back to one card. Subagent files (`.../<session>/subagents/agent-*.jsonl`) belong to the parent session. Only transcripts of catalog sessions are searched (a hit must be openable); transcripts of terminal sessions Loom never saw are out of scope (the contract §13 "import history" idea).
2. Streaming matcher: forward sequential read, newest file first (by mtime). Line splitting copies `SubagentTranscriptTail` (whole lines only, partial tail held back, 128 KiB cap; passing `coldWindowBytes: Int.max` already makes it read from byte 0). Per line: ASCII-case-folded byte gate for the needle first (like `ClaudeUsageParser.contains`), and only on a gate hit a narrow Decodable that keeps `type` (`user` or `assistant`), `timestamp`, and `message.content` text blocks (string content or `type=="text"` blocks). Skip tool_result blocks, `thinking`, `isMeta` and compact-summary lines. Re-fold the extracted text with `SessionCatalog.fold` (Turkish-aware) before the final match, so a raw-byte false positive inside a tool result is discarded. Snippet = about 160 characters around the first match (the matching line), plus role, timestamp and byte offset of the line.
3. Budgets, in the type: first pass = the newest 14 days or 512 MiB, whichever ends first, hard 2 s; a "Search older" action extends up to the CLI's retention window (cleanupPeriodDays, default 30, verified on code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-directory, configurable) with 8 s / 4 GiB. One search in flight, one utility-queue worker, `Task.checkCancellation()` per 1 MiB chunk. Numbers are placeholders until the spike below.
4. LoomCatalogUI: a `TranscriptSearchModel` (@Observable, owned beside `deletions` and `usage` in `CatalogModel`) started from `CatalogModel.setSearchText` after the existing debounce plus a second settle (about 300 ms, at least 3 characters, the search setting on), cancelled by the next keystroke and by `.conversationDeleted` frames; generation-guarded like `searchGeneration`. Hits arrive as coalesced batches (at most 5 per second, as `SubagentTranscriptFeed` does), grouped per session (2 hits then "+N"). Results re-join the catalog with the `notDeleting` fence at display time, so a session being deleted never shows a snippet.
5. Triggers are events only: a settled query typed by the user. No FSEvents consumer, no timer, nothing runs when the field is empty or the window is hidden.
6. Accurate status: the footer always states coverage ("Searched 63 of 214 sessions, last 14 days. Search older"), never a bare "No matches" after a partial pass. Drift: count text-bearing lines that fail to decode and show it the way Usage shows "N lines not understood" (`UsageDiagnostics`, Settings and diagnostics export); a profile with transcripts but zero extractable text shows "Conversation search unavailable", never wrong results.
7. Deletion: nothing to purge. `ClaudeDataDeleter` removes the transcript, and the search reads only transcripts, so deleted means unsearchable by construction. The same holds for the CLI's own retention sweep: a swept transcript simply stops matching, with no stale index.
8. Off switch: Settings → General "Search conversation text" (default on; it reads only on typing and keeps no copy). Per-project exclusion only if the user asks for it.
9. Step 0 (S, independent): add `ticket_key` to `conditions(for:)`. It is populated only for sessions an agent opened through the D-40 skill, so it helps with EDDP-123 only partly.

V2 only if measured too slow: a separate `transcript-search.sqlite3` (never the catalog, never inside `usage-index.sqlite3`) with an FTS5 table of user and assistant text only, fed by a second handler on the same watcher (fan-out in `AppDelegate+Usage`). The indexer would arm on the first search of an app run, collect changed paths while idle (the `UsageModel.transcriptsChanged` collect pattern), and catch up when the search field is focused. Rows would be purged when `ItemRemoved` arrives or the walker no longer sees the file (stricter than the usage index, which keeps history). Deletion would use a registered `ConversationForgetters` hook, `secure_delete`, a digest guard against re-index and WAL truncate, copying `ClaudeUsageIndex.forget`; it would not be one transaction with the file delete (the idea text says "same transaction", but Loom's real model is "forget hook in the verifying step, retry on throw"). The dry-run preview (`ConversationDeletionPreview`) would gain an index-rows line.

NOT in v1: "open at the right place". A live or idle terminal cannot scroll to a historic message, and `EndedScreenStore` snapshots are memory-only. Landing on the hit needs a read-only main-session transcript reader (windowed pread of about 64 KiB either side of the stored offset, rendered by the `SubagentTranscriptParser` plus `SubagentTranscriptView` path). That is the seed of the separate conversation-view idea. v1 click = select the session (record view with Resume, or the live terminal), because the snippet already answers "which session".

## Where it shows


Sidebar only, nothing new in the chrome. Typing in the always-visible search field (Cmd+F, 30 pt) first shows today's title, provider and project matches unchanged and immediately (stable order, no rows jumping). After the second settle a section "In conversations" appears below them (day-heading style, `ActivityHeading`). Each hit row is the project-stripe plus indicator slot, the session title and relative time, and under it a 2-line mono-ish snippet (secondary colour) with the match in the accent colour and a "you" or "Claude" prefix. At most 2 hits per session, then "+3 more". A footer line states coverage and holds the "Search older" link, with a small spinner while a pass runs. Click selects the session as any card click does; Return opens the first hit. Empty-state text changes from "Search covers title, provider and project" to include conversation text. The hit row is a new `LoomTheme` variant, so it needs a design preview in the repo's `design/*-preview.html` style and an update to UI-MAP.md and DESIGN.md. Not proposed: a Cmd-K palette (a new overlay surface, and Cmd+K is the terminal clear-screen habit) and Cmd+F inside the subagent panel (Edit > Find is bound to sidebar search, `AppDelegate.swift:705`; a transcript find bar can come with the reader).

## Principle check


Resources: PASS for V1. No standing state, no watcher consumer, no timer. A bounded burst only on a settled user query, with a byte and time budget, one worker, cancel on typing. RISK: page-cache churn and one core for up to 2 s while a session streams output; measure it against the CP5 1 MiB/s fixture. V2 would be a PARTIAL RISK: a persistent second copy plus a cold build of the whole corpus at about 200 MiB/s (a 3 GB corpus takes about 15 s of utility CPU), and the retention-purge logic.
Predictability: PASS if title matches stay first and stable and content hits are appended as a labelled section. Cursor-style "content ranked above titles" would make rows jump under the cursor as results stream in, so I reject it.
Accurate status: PASS only with the coverage footer. A partial scan reporting "no results" would be a false negative, which is the main violation to avoid. "Deleted" is verified by construction in V1; V2 needs the forget hook plus a failed hook keeping the deletion open.
Fluency: PASS. No confirmation, the existing field, and an optional "Search older" that is the user's call.
Modularity: PASS. A LoomCore searcher with no UI import, a separate model, no change to `ClaudeUsageIndex`. Reuse the walker and tail by extracting them to a shared file rather than adding a second walker.
CLI-update resilience: RISK, contained. The JSONL is internal and unversioned, but Loom already depends on it (usage, subagent panel) with defensive parsers. The failure mode is "coverage down" or "unavailable", surfaced like `linesNotUnderstood`, never wrong results. It reads text blocks only, not tool shapes.
Ownership: PASS. Read-only, no process started.
User authority: PASS as a user-typed query shown to the user. Do NOT expose this search to Claude sessions (for example a skill or socket verb): that would let one session read another's conversation, which is the D-43/D-46 channel and needs its own user-authorized design.
Privacy: snippets can show secrets pasted into prompts, so the section needs the off switch and it must not be logged (`LoomLog` public fields only: counts, durations). Contract §13 lists "full transcript search" as an idea without scope decision, so it needs a new D-entry before building.

## Value for the user (3/5)

The pain is real for someone with several sessions across two projects and EDDP-* tickets, and the answer is often just a snippet plus Resume. But the user's own wish list (side chat, right panel, usage, deletion, idle cost) never names transcript search; with 2-3 live sessions, the 30-day default retention and a notes vault the recall window is short; and Claude's own `--resume` picker takes a search term. Useful and cheap in V1, not transformative. The M estimate holds only for V1 (about 700-900 lines in LoomCore plus model, sidebar section, design preview, tests, evidence). The FTS5 variant is L (the reference size is `ClaudeUsageIndex` at 1,318 lines plus forgetters, eviction, schema, settings and diagnostics), and the hit reader is another M-L. Orca's L was the honest figure for the index.

## Experiments before building

- S1, user-run, 1 minute, decides the budgets: `du -sk ~/.claude/projects`, the .jsonl file count, and the byte total of files modified in the last 14 and 30 days (including any CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR profile). I may not read the real ~/.claude. Rule: up to about 1.5 GB inside retention means a full scan within budget and no 'search older' tier needed; 1.5-6 GB means two-tier budgets as designed; above about 6 GB or p95 over 3 s warm means build V2 (FTS5).
- S2, scan throughput, run only when the user says the Mac is idle (repo rule), on the existing generated corpus (`ClaudeUsageCorpusTests`, 1 GiB, outside ~/Documents, isolated, deleted after) with a CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR fixture: the fold-aware byte gate plus text-only decode, warm and with the cache purged, ms per GiB, RSS delta, and then the same while the CP5 1 MiB/s terminal fixture streams (the 40% of one core ceiling and frame drops). The corpus is synthetic, so also check the hit rate of the byte gate on realistic lines (tool_result-heavy). Decides: pass at or under 1.5 s per GiB warm and no visible stall.
- S3, only if S1 or S2 fail: FTS5 in the system SQLite on macOS 27 (`CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE ... fts5`); tokenizer behaviour for Turkish (İ/ı folding vs `loom_fold`), ticket keys (`tokenchars '-'` vs trigram) and DB bytes per GB of transcripts; contentless_delete availability. Decides between a stored-snippet table and an offset-only table.

## Questions for the user

- Is searching conversation text on by default, with a Settings switch? (It keeps no copy, but snippets can show secrets you pasted into prompts.)
- Does a hit that matches both title and text show once with a snippet under the card, or in two sections? (My default is two sections, so the title results stay stable.)
- Do you want 'open at the right place' enough to fund the read-only main-session transcript reader (M-L, shared with a conversation view), or is snippet plus Resume enough for v2?
- Is searching only Loom-catalogued sessions acceptable, with transcripts of plain terminal sessions out of scope until an 'import history' decision?
- Is the 14-day / 512 MiB first pass the right default window, or do you want everything within the 30-day retention on every query (decided by S1)?
- Skip the Cmd-K palette and the Cmd+F in-panel find in v2?
