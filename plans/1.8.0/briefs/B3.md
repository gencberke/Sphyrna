# B3: the status bar

**Goal.** A 26 pt status bar runs along the whole window's bottom. It has one usage segment per connected harness that reports
limits, and on its trailing side the working count, Keep Mac Awake and notices. It replaces the sidebar's footer chip
(FEEDBACK-6 FB6-3; FEEDBACK-7 Q7). It is modelled on Orca's (`orca/src/renderer/src/components/status-bar/StatusBarSurface.tsx`).

**Starts after** B1, B2 (the footer's room), C2 (limits per harness from the catalogue).

**Read.**
- DESIGN v4 "The status bar"; B0's window with the bar and its popover.
- In `v2`:
  - `Sources/LoomCatalogUI/SidebarFooter.swift`, `UsageChipContent.swift`, `UsageModel.swift` (C2's version),
    `UsageLimitCards.swift`, `MenuBarState.swift`;
  - the window's root layout (find where the split view and the sidebar are built: `MainWindow*.swift` or `RootView*.swift`);
  - Keep Mac Awake's setting (DESIGN §6 General; `grep -rn "keepAwake\|KeepAwake" Sources`).
- Orca's `StatusBarSurface.tsx`, `StatusBarProviderSegment.tsx`, `InlineProviderUsage.tsx`, `CaffeinateStatusSegment.tsx`.

**Write set.**
- New `Sources/LoomCatalogUI/StatusBar*.swift` and `LoomTheme+StatusBar.swift`;
- the window's root layout file (only to place the bar);
- `SidebarView.swift` (only to remove the footer's slot);
- `SidebarFooter.swift` and `UsageChipContent.swift` (removed or folded into the bar);
- their tests and DESIGN's lines.

**Approach.**
1. **The bar:**
   - 26 high under both the sidebar and the content, the sidebar plane's colour, a hairline on top, caption type;
   - hidden in full screen with the menu bar.
2. **Usage segments**, one per connected harness with a reading:
   - the mark, a 40 × 4 meter of the tightest window, and each window's "N% · time to reset";
   - nothing new is asked: the bar reads `UsageModel`;
   - a click opens a 360-wide popover (v4 floating) with each window's full pace line (reuse `PlanPaceLine`), when it was read,
     Check Again and Open Usage;
   - a harness with no reading shows its mark and "No limits read".
   - the segments iterate the catalogue (`UsageModel.usageHarnesses`, the connect switch): C2 left `UsageModel.rebuildChip`,
     `paces` and `UsageChipContent` naming Claude and Codex, and the bar replaces them.
3. **The trailing side:**
   - "N working" (a click lists them in a small menu and selects one);
   - Keep Mac Awake as a toggle bound to the same preference as Settings;
   - a notice slot for "update available" from Connections.
4. **Cost:**
   - the bar is redrawn only when a figure it shows changes;
   - no timers;
   - no animation while the app is not active;
   - the relative times ("3h 39m") are recomputed on the existing minute tick, if one exists, or when the window becomes
     active.
5. **Accessibility:** each segment is one element ("Claude, 5-hour 96% left, resets in 3 hours 39 minutes").

**Done when.**
- The bar shows Claude and Codex segments with fake readings, the popover opens and closes with Esc, and the toggle matches
  Settings.
- The sidebar has no footer chip.
- The energy proxy (the render probe or a redraw counter) shows no redraw at rest over 60 s.

**Checks.** The old footer's suites (`SidebarFooterTests`, `UsageHarnessTests`, `ContextPaceTests`), moved to the bar or deleted
with their files, and new `StatusBarTests` (segments per reading, the popover's content, no redraw without a change); then
`--tests`. Unverified: energy at rest (gate).
