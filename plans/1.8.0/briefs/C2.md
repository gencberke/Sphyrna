# C2: everything that lists harnesses reads the catalogue

**Goal.** After C1, every place that lists, probes or gates harnesses iterates `HarnessCatalogue` filtered by availability:
- models (with each model's own effort levels and default effort);
- the usage and limits probes;
- Home's limits card;
- the new-session menus and entries;
- the palette;
- the menu bar and the Dock menu.

It also closes the optimizer's items 2, 4 and 10. This is FEEDBACK-6 FB6-13 step 5 and FB6-8's data part.

**Read.**
- C1's report and its files; FEEDBACK-6 FB6-8 and "Proposals" FB6-7/8 ("Beneath it").
- `evidence/r6/optimizer/REPORT.md` items 2, 4 and 10 (read them whole: they name the lines and the tests to add).
- In `v2`:
  - `Sources/LoomCore/ModelCatalog.swift`, `HarnessProbe.swift`, `HarnessProbeClaude.swift`, `HarnessProbeCodex.swift`;
  - `ClaudePlanLimits.swift`, `HarnessHealth.swift`;
  - `Sources/LoomCatalogUI/UsageModel.swift`, `HomeConnect.swift`, `UsageLimitCards.swift`, `SidebarPeek.swift`,
    `PaletteSources.swift`, `CatalogModel+NewSession.swift`, `MenuBarState.swift`;
  - `Sources/loom-app/AppDelegate+Usage.swift`.
- For the effort rule: Orca `src/shared/agent-effort-level.ts` (a level is valid by its shape, so a level a newer CLI adds still
  passes).

**Write set.**
- The `LoomCore` files above.
- `UsageModel.swift`, `HomeConnect.swift` (data only), `UsageLimitCards.swift` (data only), `SidebarPeek.swift`,
  `PaletteSources.swift`, `CatalogModel+NewSession.swift` (the entries list), `MenuBarState.swift`.
- `CatalogModel.swift`: only to hold `ModelCatalog` and `SidebarPeekStore`.
- `AppDelegate+Usage.swift`.
- Their tests; SPEC §6.7 and §12.2's probe lines.

Not yours: visual layout (B2, B3, C3), the draft's types (C4).

**Approach.**
1. **`ModelCatalogEntry`** carries `harness`, the model's id and full name, a one-line description if the CLI gives one, the
   context size if known, `efforts: [String]` and `defaultEffort`.
   - Claude's models and their levels come from the CLI as today; Codex's from the app-server model list (P0 confirms the
     fields).
   - opencode, agy and pi give entries only where P1 found a list that needs no model call; otherwise none.
   - The catalogue is gated by the connect switch and availability.
2. **Effort tokens** are validated by shape (`^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}$`), never by a fixed five-level list.
3. **One probe outcome** (optimizer 2): answered, failed (with a reason), or cached. A failed Claude check reads "Could not
   check", never "Not signed in". The API-key account branch works once the CLI's fields are seen; leave it unknown if not.
4. **One source for limits** (optimizer 4): `UsageModel`'s limits derive from the probe's reading, nil included. Drop the
   no-probe path. Share the window ids as constants.
5. **No singletons** (optimizer 10): `ModelCatalog` and `SidebarPeekStore` live on `CatalogModel` and are passed down. No view
   reads a `.shared`.
6. **Every list** of harnesses (menus, palette entries such as "New Codex Session", the menu bar, the Dock menu, Home's limits)
   iterates the connected catalogue, so a harness turned off disappears from them and Not found never appears as startable.
7. **The Codex probe** is asked on activation and each minute under the 5-minute floor (D-103 Q6). Nothing new asks a model.

**Done when.**
- Turning a harness off or on in preferences changes every list above, with no restart.
- The optimizer's tests exist and pass:
  - a timed-out first Claude check gives "Could not check";
  - account A with limits, then account B with none, leaves `limits` nil.
- No `ModelCatalog.shared` or `SidebarPeekStore.shared` remains.
- `ModelCatalogEntry.efforts` is filled for Claude and Codex models from the CLIs.

**Checks.** The suites of every touched file (`UsageHarnessTests`, the probe tests, `HarnessHealth` tests, palette and menu
tests), new tests for 1–6; then `--tests`. Unverified: the real Codex model list (gate, after P0).
