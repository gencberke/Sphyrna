# B4: the model picker and the effort picker

**Goal.** Choosing a model and choosing its effort become two pickers side by side ([D-106](../../../DECISIONS.md)):
- the model chip opens the model panel (search, a rail of harness marks, every model grouped by harness);
- the effort chip opens a short menu with only the levels the chosen model offers;
- a model with no levels shows no effort chip.

This replaces the 332 pt popover (`ComposerModelPopover`). It is FEEDBACK-6 FB6-7 and FB6-8. D-106 replaces D-103 Q7's option B
(the effort as a second step on the same panel) with the two pickers.

**Approach.**
1. **The chips:**
   - two ghost buttons, 28 high: `[mark] <model> ⌄` and `[effort icon] <level> ⌄`;
   - the open chip takes the selected fill;
   - the effort chip is absent when the model has no `efforts`.
2. **The model panel:** a popover under its chip, 412 wide, at elevation 3, keyboard-first (focus starts in the search field).
   - **The rail (44):** marks only, with the name and state in the help tag:
     - Recent (the last five choices across harnesses);
     - the catalogue's harnesses that are on;
     - under a hairline, the ones that are Off or Not found, dimmed with a dot.

     It follows the list's scroll, and a click jumps to that harness.
   - **The list:** every model grouped by harness: a header with the mark, the label and the count, then one 32 pt row per model.
     - A row holds the name, a description line and the context size in mono.
     - The highlighted row has the selected fill; the current model has the accent check.
     - Off and Not found harnesses end the list as one row each, with "Install…" or "Turn On…", which opens Settings →
       Connections.
   - **Search** filters across the harnesses that are on; a result row shows its harness's mark, and a line says which
     harnesses were not searched.
   - **Keys:** ↑↓ moves, ↵ chooses and closes, Esc closes without a change.
3. **The effort menu:** a popover under its chip, 300 wide, at elevation 3.
   - A header: "Effort" and the model's name.
   - One row per level of the chosen model: the level, a one-line note, "Default" on the model's default, and the accent check
     on the current level.
   - ↑↓ and ↵ choose; Esc closes.
4. **Changing the model** keeps the effort when the new model offers that level, and otherwise takes the new model's default.
5. **Applying:** a choice writes `HarnessLaunchChoices` through C4's rules (a model from another harness switches the draft's
   harness). In a running session's header, the model panel shows the session's harness only and applies as today: the next
   session or the CLI's own `/model`. Write which in the report.
6. **Accessibility:**
   - both popovers are menus;
   - the rail and the list are lists;
   - the effort menu announces "Effort for <model>";
   - each chip says its value ("Model, Opus 5.5", "Effort, High").

**Done when.**
- From the draft, a user can pick a Codex model with the keyboard alone (model chip, type, ↵) and the draft switches to Codex.
  The effort chip then shows that model's own levels, and its default when the old level is not among them.
- A model without levels hides the effort chip.
- Esc in either popover leaves the choice unchanged.
- Off and Not found harnesses cannot be chosen, and they offer their action.
- `ComposerModelPopover` is gone, or kept only by the frozen Chat code; say which.
