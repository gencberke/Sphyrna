- a lifted plane with the search field and nav rows;
- sentence-case section headings with icon buttons;
- project heads;
- the new session card without a leading status column, its status at the top line's trailing end (FEEDBACK-7 FB7-1).

DESIGN's sidebar lines.

**Approach.**
1. **Plane:** the sidebar draws the Sidebar token, and the default fill becomes Raised (`UIPreferences.swift`: B1's tokens are in, its default was outside B1's write set). The existing fill and opacity
   setting keeps working.
2. **Top:**
   - the search field (32, full width, opening the palette as today);
   - nav rows for Home (with a badge counting sessions that need you) and New Session (`⌘N` key chip).
3. **Headings:** Small medium secondary, with the view's icon buttons at the trailing end. No mono capitals.
4. **The card:** the three lines of DESIGN v4.
   - **Top line:** the project mark and name (Activity view) or the harness mark and model (Projects view), then the status
     at the trailing end. Working is the spinner, "Working" and the elapsed time in mono. Needs you, Failed and Finished are a
     dot and the word in the status's text colour. Ready shows the time since the last activity in mono tertiary.
   - **Title:** at most two lines.
   - **Bottom line:** the branch in mono with the harness mark at the trailing end.
   - **Reason line:** under the title in the Activity view.
   - **States:** no fill at rest, the hover fill, and selected raised with the strong hairline at elevation 2 (Q3).
5. **The elapsed time** ticks once a second only for a visible, working card in an active window. Reuse the working
   indicator's gating (`realSidebarRunsAWorkingRowOnlyForAnActiveWindowAndExpandedSidebar`). No timer runs for a hidden or idle
   card.
6. **Subagents:** they branch from the mark's column. The branch line starts under the mark's centre. Rows stay 24 with the
   still running mark.
7. **Draft rows and Peek** keep their behaviour in v4's look.

**Done when.**
- Both views draw the v4 sidebar in Ink and Paper. A Ready card has no empty column at its leading edge.
- Every status reads in words at the trailing end. The selected card is neutral raised, and the project tone stays on its
  mark and tab.
- The render probe shows no extra redraws at rest, and the working-indicator window test's rule still holds (the gate runs
  it).
- Screenshots in `evidence/r9/b2/` match B0's windows.
