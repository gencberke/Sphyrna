# B5a, B5b, B5c: the surfaces in v4

Three slices with one method and disjoint write sets. They can run side by side, one implementer each. Each starts after B1
(and B5c after C3).

**Goal.** Every surface outside the sidebar, the status bar, Connections and the model panel takes Ink v4:
- v4's controls and type;
- the planes and elevations;
- the copy rules of DESIGN v4 §5.

Layouts change only where this brief says so. The surfaces read as one family with B0's windows.

**Read (all three).**
- `design/DESIGN.md` (v4 rules and §3, §6) and `reference.html`'s v4 chapter; your surfaces' own DESIGN sections (below).
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §11 for your surfaces' files, accessibility ids and evidence keys.
- Orca's UX rules for review: `~/Documents/github/orca/docs/styleguide/ux-review-rubric.md`. Use it to check your screens
  before the report.

**Method (all three).**
1. List your surfaces' controls and map each to a v4 role and size (button roles, fields, switches, segmented controls,
   badges, key chips). Write that list into the report.
2. Replace bespoke controls with B1's shared ones. A control that cannot be replaced without a layout change is kept and listed.
3. Apply v4's type roles; section labels become Label (SF Pro capitals), and mono stays for machine text only.
4. Apply the copy rules: no filler words; result verbs only after results; Cancel quiet; every empty or missing-data state
   offers its action.
5. Take evidence screenshots (fake providers) of every surface in Ink and Paper, before and after, into `evidence/r9/<id>/`.
