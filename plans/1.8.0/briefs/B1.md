# B1: Ink v4 tokens and the control family

**Goal.** Move the app's tokens and shared controls to Ink v4 as `@user` approved them in B0's reference. After this slice,
every surface that uses the shared tokens and controls already looks closer to v4. The surface slices (B2, B3, B5a–c) then
change layouts.

**Starts only after** `@user` has approved B0 and the planner has written the approved values into `design/DESIGN.md` (§0, Ink v4)
on `v2` (done 27 Oct, D-106). Read that section, not DESIGN-V4.md, for the values.

**Read.**
- `design/DESIGN.md`: the v4 rules at its top, §0.1 (tokens), §0.3 (shape, size, depth), §0.4 (components), and §3 (the v3
  values you replace); `design/reference.html#ink4` (the approved
  windows).
- In `v2`, `Sources/LoomCatalogUI/`:
  - every `LoomTheme*.swift` (start with `LoomTheme.swift`, `LoomTheme+Surface*`, `LoomTheme+Status.swift`,
    `LoomTheme+Sheets.swift`);
  - `ControlButtonStyles.swift` (`ControlButtonStyle.Kind`: primary, secondary, ghost, destructive, tinted);
  - `ChromeButtonStyle.swift`, `SettingsControls.swift`;
  - `RowHighlight.swift` (the pill), `SheetFamily.swift`, the popover host (`InkHost`).
- `Tests/LoomCatalogUITests/ThemeAppearanceTests.swift`, `LoomThemeTests.swift`, `HighlightTests.swift`,
  `ProjectColorUITests.swift`.
- `scripts/check-theme-literals.sh`.

**Write set.**
- `Sources/LoomCatalogUI/LoomTheme*.swift`;
- `ControlButtonStyles.swift`, `ChromeButtonStyle.swift`, `SettingsControls.swift`, `RowHighlight.swift`, `SheetFamily.swift`, the
  popover host's style;
- new shared control files (`InkField.swift`, `InkSwitch.swift`, `InkSegmented.swift`, `InkBadge.swift`, `KeyChip.swift`);
- the theme tests.

Not yours: any surface's layout, the sidebar's structure (B2), the status bar (B3).

**Approach.**
1. **Tokens:** set the v4 values for Ink and Paper and recompute the Warm and Cool casts from the new neutrals. Keep the token
   names; add new ones for Sidebar, Muted, Input edge, Input fill and the three elevations. A token that no longer has a use is
   removed in the same commit.
2. **Buttons:** `ControlButtonStyle` gets the six roles (default, secondary, outline, ghost, link, destructive) and four sizes
   (24, 28, 32, 36). Map today's kinds:
   - primary → default (now the neutral fill);
   - secondary → secondary or outline, by where it is used;
   - ghost → ghost;
   - destructive → destructive;
   - tinted → outline or secondary.

   A call site that needs a different role than the mapping gives is listed in the report, not changed here.
3. **The focus ring:** the 3 pt accent halo of DESIGN §0.1, drawn on keyboard focus, on every control style.
4. **Shared controls:** the field, switch, segmented control, badge and key chip of DESIGN §0.4. Existing call sites switch to
   them where it is a drop-in change. Call sites that need layout work are left to B5 and listed.
5. **Elevation:** three shadow tokens. Remove every other shadow value; the composer and popovers use them.
6. **Type:** add the Label (SF Pro 11 semibold capitals, tracking 5 %) and Caption roles. Switch the section-label call sites
   from mono to Label: they are few, and DESIGN lists them.

**Done when.**
- The tokens match DESIGN §0.1 in Ink and Paper and every cast, and every contrast floor holds in the tests (a floor that fails
  is reported with its numbers; do not loosen a test).
- Every button in the app renders in one of the six roles.
- Evidence screenshots of Home, a session with the inspector, Settings → General and a sheet, in Ink and Paper, are in
  `evidence/r9/b1/`. They come from evidence mode with fake providers (DEVELOPMENT §5), before and after.

**Checks.** `--suite ThemeAppearanceTests LoomThemeTests HighlightTests ProjectColorUITests` and the suites of the call sites
you touched; then `--tests`. Unverified: hover and focus on screen (gate).
