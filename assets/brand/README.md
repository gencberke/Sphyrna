# Brand artwork

Harness marks identify the selected third-party tool. They are not Loom branding or endorsements. Original colors, shapes and aspect ratios are retained. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Sources retrieved 2026-09-22:

- `claude.svg`: **Claude Spark – Clay**, from Anthropic's [official press kit](https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit), under `Anthropic media resources/Anthropic logos/Claude logos/3 Claude Spark/SVG/`. Copyright Anthropic.
- `codex.png`: official dark-color Codex app icon, copied without modification from the installed OpenAI app (26.915.31945)'s `Contents/Resources/icon-codex-dark-color.png`. Copyright OpenAI. [Brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/). It was the Codex harness's mark until 20 Oct 2026 and is kept as a record.
- `chatgpt.png` (retrieved 2026-10-20): the ChatGPT app icon from the installed ChatGPT app (26.1013.40934)'s `Contents/Resources/icon-chatgpt.png`, downscaled from 2048 to 1024 px and otherwise unmodified. Copyright OpenAI. [Brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/). Since 20 Oct 2026 it is the Codex harness's mark (user decision): `../harness/harness-codex.png` renders it.
- `opencode.svg`: dark-background square logo from [OpenCode's official repository](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/blob/dev/packages/console/app/src/asset/brand/opencode-logo-dark-square.svg), distributed on its [brand page](https://opencode.ai/brand). Copyright Anomaly.
- `antigravity.png`: full-color icon from [Google Antigravity press assets](https://www.antigravity.google/press), [original PNG](https://www.antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.png). Copyright Google.

`../harness/` holds transparent 128 px PNG renders of these marks for the 14–32 pt UI at Retina scale.

`../harness/harness-codex-template.png` is the knot alone, cut from `chatgpt.png`: the light background is removed by luminance and the knot's shape is unchanged, black on transparent, so it can be drawn in the text colour (a template image). Ink (v3) draws Codex's mark this way, after Orca's sidebar (user decision, 20 Oct 2026); the app keeps `harness-codex.png` until its provider icon draws template images.

The Loom app icon (`../Loom.*`, `../Loom-mark.svg`, `../Loom-menubar.svg`) is original user-supplied artwork, preserved from Herdr Desktop; only the SVG accessibility label was renamed from "Herdr" to "Loom" (user decision, 6 Oct 2026). Ink, the design language since 20 Oct 2026 (D-74), keeps this mark until it is redrawn: a hammerhead is planned for the app icon, the menu bar mark and About, and the name may change too (O-13).
