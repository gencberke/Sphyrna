2 · Subagents under their session visible in both views; read-only

Data Loom can rely on (research of 12 Oct, real Claude 2.1.293): per subagent its type (Explore, implementer…), a short description, foreground or background, running / finished, start and finish time, depth, and its tokens. Not available: success versus failure, the tool it is running. Clicking a subagent row selects the parent session (there is no separate terminal for a subagent). Finished subagents of the last turn fold into one line; the next prompt clears them.
