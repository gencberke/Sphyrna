  1. A1The head sits on the window's header line, where a session's title would be: the project's mark (26), the name in title type and the folder in mono, which you can select. There is no bar of its own.
  2. A2The stats count what the page holds: sessions with a run under way, all of the project's sessions, the week's tokens (only above 0) and the folder's branch.
  3. A3View tabs, underlined: Sessions, Branches and Instructions. The page opens on Sessions every time.
  4. A4A session row reads like the sidebar's: the status, the harness's mark, the title and its last line (the reason Sphyrna holds, else the end of the transcript), the branch and the time. A click shows the session.
  5. A5Presets: a first message with its mode and model. The mode's icon in its tone, the name, and the model after its harness's mark; the message is in the tooltip. A click opens a new session with it and starts nothing.
  6. A6The past seven days from the usage index: tokens, the sessions with usage, and the estimate.
  7. A7While its page is open, the project's head in the sidebar takes the neutral selected fill; the selected session keeps its raised card.
  8. A8⋯ in the page header is the project menu: new sessions with their harness's mark, the colour as seven swatches, the place in the list, and Remove Empty Project.
