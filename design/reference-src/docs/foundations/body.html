06

The school

The sharks are Sphyrna's identity, not decoration. The hammerhead is the main character; a thresher and a whale shark swim with it, and the remora rides with it as Remora's face. Each has a place in the app's flow, and none of them ever swims behind something you are reading.

Hammerhead the main character

The first launch, Home's band, a new session, About and the waiting panes. It is the app icon, Rising, and it brings the remora to Home.

Thresher the quick one

Its tail is as long as its body. It marks an empty Changes tab.

Remora Remora's face

Known by its disc: an oval of plates, a bar across its broad, flat head for each. It rides on the hammerhead's flank and rests at the start of Home's brief.

Whale shark the slow giant

It swims deep below the first launch and a new session, and marks an empty Side tab. Its spots are the only pattern in the app.
