01

Review is wide

Changes opens the inspector at 556, so a diff reads at its length, and a subagent's transcript opens there too. Overview and the Agents list keep 328, and Side keeps 392. The width follows your click and never animates.

02

A diff is content

Old and new line numbers, a sign, and a band of the success or failure hue on each changed line. There is no syntax colour, so the bands are the diff's only colour, and the text holds 7:1 on them.

03

Comments go back as one message

The "+" in the gutter, or a click on a line, opens a comment under it. Send to Claude puts every comment of the scope in one plain message and sends it the way Return does.

04

A transcript in two voices

What the agent says reads in the chat's type. Its tools and their results stay in mono, and quieter. Claude's own marks stay: ⏺ when it says or does something, ⎿ for the answer, ✻ while it thinks.

05

Filled means Return

A sheet's filled button is its default, and only a sheet that saves has one. Delete, Remove, Discard and Rewind draw theirs tinted: Return does nothing there, Esc cancels, and the focus starts on Cancel.

06

One sheet shape

420 wide, or 480 when it lists files. The question is the title, the session's name sits under it, then come the facts, and the buttons sit at the trailing edge with Cancel first. A way out, such as Remove from Sphyrna Only, sits at the leading edge.
