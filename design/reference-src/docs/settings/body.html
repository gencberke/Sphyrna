01

One window, six tabs

Settings is a 680×520 window that follows the appearance and the tone. Its sections are view tabs in the title band, where the window's title would be: General, Theme, Usage, Sessions, Remora and About.

02

Groups are cards

A group is a card on raised 1 under a section label. A row holds its label, one line of help and its control at the trailing edge, and rows divide by hairlines.

03

One control family

Switches, segments, menu pickers, steppers and buttons are the main window's: 26 high, the same radii, hover and focus ring. A risky choice asks first.

04

A tone is a cast

Neutral, Warm and Cool shift every grey level of the window a few degrees, the sidebar and Settings included. Status, accent, project and mode colours stay as they are.

05

Glass is the sidebar's

Glass draws the sidebar's canvas at 0.8 over the desktop's material. Solid is the default; nothing else is glass, and Reduce Transparency draws Solid.

06

About is quiet

The app icon, the name and the version, then the facts in mono. The app icon sits above the facts, and nothing else is drawn on this page.
