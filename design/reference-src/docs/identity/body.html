01

One name

Sphyrna is the hammerhead's genus, from the Greek sphyra, a hammer. One word, no pun, written the same in every language.

02

The icon is the art

The app's own ASCII engine draws the icon: the same field, ramp and eyes as Home's band and the new-session page, held still. Rising: the hammerhead climbing to the left on black.

03

A mark for the menu bar

Front, the hammerhead seen from the front: the hammer with an eye at each tip, the dorsal fin, the jaw and the pectoral fins, in one colour on a 20 pt canvas.

04

Remora arrives on Home

Home's assistant is Remora. When Home opens with a new brief, the hammerhead brings the remora in, lets it go at the start of the brief and swims on. The remora stays there, beside its sentence.

05

One drawing everywhere

First launch, About, the alerts, the Dock and the banners show the same hammerhead. The art keeps its rules: it never swims behind something you read.

06

What changes

Sphyrna is written wherever the app is named, from 1.6.0. The code's names (LoomCore and the rest) stay. The bundle id, the data folder and the repository keep Loom's names, and the wordmark is set in SF Pro (D-83).

03

The menu bar mark

Front: the hammerhead seen from the front, in one colour on a 20 pt canvas, 18 wide. macOS tints it white or black with the bar, and a status badge cuts into its lower right corner. The table shows it at real size in every state.

Front the menu bar mark

The hammer with an eye at each tip, the dorsal fin above, the jaw and the pectoral fins below. The eyes are holes in the hammer, so the bar shows through them.

  1. Canvas20 by 20 pt, the mark 18 wide, as LoomTheme.MenuBar draws the menu bar mark. The paths use only M, L, C and Z, the commands the app's own path reader takes.
  2. BadgeA 7 pt dot at the lower right, cut 5.2 around: needs you, finished or failed. Working draws the braille spinner in the same slot (§4).
  3. ColourIdle, it is a template image and macOS tints it. With a badge the app draws it: white on a dark bar, black on a light one.
  4. Not ASCIIAt 16 pt a character cell is one or two pixels wide, so the menu bar gets a drawn mark; the art's characters start at the icon.
