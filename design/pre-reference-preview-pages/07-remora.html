Remora

Home's assistant. Remora reads your sessions and says where you left off. It answers what you ask about them, and it remembers what you tell it to. It only reads: an answer links to sessions and can leave an unsent line in a composer, and Remora never sends, starts or stops anything (D-64).

01

It only reads

An answer links to the sessions it names, and Put in draft leaves an unsent line in a composer. Remora never sends a message, never starts or stops a session, and never answers a request (D-64).

02

Its words, Sphyrna's frame

Remora's sentences are the model's, in first person and in your question's language. The heading, the tiles' catalog lines, the states and the buttons stay in Sphyrna's plain voice.

03

One home, two doors

Remora lives on Home. Outside Home it has two doors only: asking from ⌘K, and a brief for a new session. Nothing else waits on it, and with Remora off both doors are gone and nothing else changes.

04

It shows what it read

Every brief and answer ends with what Remora read, how long it took and its sources. A session it could not read is named as unread, never summarised from nothing.

05

Memory you can see

The working memory stays in Sphyrna's data folder; what lasts is one note, Remora.md, in your vault. The memory sheet shows both, and any line can go. Remora saves a fact only when you ask, or confirm one.

06

The remora is its face

It comes in on the hammerhead's flank with a new brief, swims while Remora reads, and holds still beside the sentence once it is written (D-77). Its disc, a bar across its head for each plate, makes it a remora at any size. It never speaks: there are no fish in the copy.

The remora's disc · round 2
The folded brief's lineBeside the briefOn the hammerhead's flank Before Now

A remora is the fish with a sucker on its head: a flat, oval disc of plates that it holds on with. Small and alone, the old drawing read as any slender fish. Now the art draws the disc, a bar across the head for each plate, on a broader and flatter head, as it draws the whale shark by its spots; a large remora also shows the ridge between the plates. The folded line's remora is a little larger and lies level, in semibold glyphs, so its disc has room. Everything else about it stays as D-77 drew it.

05

Beyond Home

Remora lives on Home. We looked for places outside it where Remora would really help, without making the app lean on it, and kept two: asking from ⌘K, and a brief for a new session. Both are doors, not new surfaces: nothing else in the app waits for Remora, and with Remora off both are gone. Everywhere else we looked, and why it stays out, is at the end.
