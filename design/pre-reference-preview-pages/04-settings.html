Settings

Everything Loom lets you set, in one small window, and About at its end. Ink draws it like the main window: view tabs in the title band, groups as cards, one control family. The pane tones become casts of Ink's greys, and Glass stays the sidebar's only material.

01

One window, five tabs

Settings is a 680×520 window that follows the appearance and the tone. Its sections are view tabs in the title band, where the window's title would be: General, Theme, Usage, Sessions and About.

02

Groups are cards

A group is a card on raised 1 under a section label. A row holds its label, one line of help and its control at the trailing edge, and rows divide by hairlines.

03

One control family

Switches, segments, menu pickers, steppers and buttons are the main window's: 26 high, the same radii, hover and focus ring. A risky choice asks first.

04

A tone is a cast

Neutral, Warm and Cool shift every grey level of the window a few degrees, the sidebar and Settings included. Status, accent, project and mode colours stay as they are.

05

Glass is the sidebar's

Glass draws the sidebar's canvas at 0.6 over the desktop's material. Nothing else is glass, and Reduce Transparency draws Solid.

06

About is quiet

The app icon, the name and the version, then the facts in mono. A hammerhead crosses once behind the icon when About opens, then rests.

04

What to react to

The content is Settings and About as built (UI-MAP → Settings); this preview draws them in Ink and settles DESIGN §5's two questions. A yes, a no or "more like X" on each is enough.

  1. Tones as casts. Neutral, Warm and Cool shift every grey of the window, the sidebar included, and leave every colour that means something. Keep the three, or only Ink's greys?
  2. The names. Neutral, Warm and Cool replace Night fleece, Workshop and Instrument. Plain enough?
  3. Glass over the black canvas. The sidebar's canvas at 0.6 over the desktop (try both wallpapers). Keep 0.6, or a deeper 0.8?
  4. Solid by default. Ink's sidebar starts Solid, and Glass stays a choice. The code starts on Glass today. Right?
  5. Tabs in the title band. The sections as underlined view tabs where the title would be, instead of a segmented control. Right?
  6. Usage. The four cards as built, in Ink, plan limits first. Anything to cut or move?
  7. About. The app icon with a hammerhead that crosses once and rests, the version in mono, three facts. The right amount?
