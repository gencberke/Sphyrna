Chat

A Claude session read as a conversation, in Ink. The behaviour is the chat spec's and stays as built; what changes is how it reads. Prose is the page, work is a quiet line you can open, and what needs you sits right above where you type.

01

Prose reads, work whispers

Claude's answer is the page: 14 on 22, on the canvas, with no bubble. Each tool step is one quiet 26 pt line that opens in place, and a settled turn folds its work into one line.

02

Your words on the right

Your message is the only bubble, a soft fill on the right. What happened to it (waiting, from the queue, not delivered) is one small line under it, and its actions appear on hover.

03

One live line

A running turn ends in one row: the spinner, what Claude does now and the clock from your send. Claude's latest thought sits above it in italics. Nothing else in the stream moves.

04

Requests sit where you act

An approval, a question or a plan pins between the stream and the composer, one at a time, with an orange edge. Return never answers it, and the stream keeps one line of what you chose.

05

One composer

The draft's card, docked, with the same bar: the mode in its tone, the context ring, and one chip for the harness, the model and its effort. Send says what Return does now and becomes a word when that is not a plain send.

06

Asides stay aside

A quick question floats over the stream on the popover level, and a side chat lives in the inspector. Neither reaches the session unless you send an answer back as a note.

    if order.status != "paid":
        return False
    return now - order.paid_at <= timedelta(days=REFUND_DAYS)
