The edges

What Sphyrna shows when there is little to show, or when something stopped: the first launch, a Mac without Claude, a session that has ended, the banners above a session, and the places outside the window, the menu bar, a notification and the Dock.

01

Say what happened

An edge says what is true, what Sphyrna did or did not do, and what you can do, in a sentence or two. No metaphor, no exclamation marks, and nothing cheerful at your expense.

02

One way on

Each edge offers the one action that moves you on: Add Project…, Check Again, Resume. It is filled only where it is the page's main action; everything else is a secondary button.

03

Ended is not failed

A session that ended has no pill and no mark, and what it left stays readable. Only a failure, a stop Sphyrna could not verify, or a problem takes the failure colour.

04

Banners stack above

A banner sits under the header, one at a time, with the rest behind a "+N" peek. It never covers the terminal or the chat, and it comes in over 200 ms by opacity only.

05

Outside, the same marks

The menu bar, its menu and the Dock read the sidebar's status column: the spinner, the attention dot, the unviewed dot and the failure dot. A notification names the session and says why.

06

Art only where nothing is read

The school roams behind the first launch, one hammerhead circles Home's band, and a small one swims where Sphyrna waits, also under Reduce Motion. None swims behind a problem, an ended screen, a banner or a menu.

First launch and Home

Home is where Sphyrna opens, so its edges are the first thing you see: the first launch, a Mac without Claude, a day with nothing waiting, and the morning after a quit with sessions running. Home's hammerhead, on one loop

The hammerhead never leaves the band. It swims left along the top and back along the bottom, and turns out of sight past the edges: as its tail leaves on one side, its head comes back on the other. The loop repeats, so the app can work out its frames once and then only draw them, with nothing simulated while Home is open; the art's spike (§5) measures which way is cheaper. It draws only while Home is on screen in an active window.

The art under Reduce Motion
Animated artThe drawings on Home, empty pages and waiting panes move, also when Reduce Motion is on. Off, each stands still on one frame.

Settings → Theme, under Appearance, on by default. The art moves under Reduce Motion, as the working spinner does (D-12): it is slow and faint, and never behind what you read. This switch stops it; then Remora's arrival is its fade. Reduce Motion still makes the transitions instant. The page plays the art the same way, whatever macOS says, and the top bar's Sharks switch is this switch.

What a session leaves when its run is over: the terminal's last screen with one line above it, the record when Sphyrna has no screen to show, and the chat, which keeps its composer. Resume plays the way back in. The record's states are the ones it explains in words.

03

Banners

A banner says what happened outside the content: something did not happen, Sphyrna is limited, or a note for you. In Ink they are Phase 2's banner everywhere, under the header as above the composer: one at a time, by weight, with the rest behind "+N". Open the stack, close the top one.

Click "+2" to open the stack. × closes a banner at once, and the next comes in over 200 ms.

Attention · Sphyrna is limited
New sessions are turned off because Sphyrna's process helper is missing. Without it Sphyrna cannot make sure Stop and Quit end all of a session's background work. Reinstall Sphyrna to fix this.

Quit, when a stop is not verified

Sphyrna could not verify that its processes stopped.

Remaining: 1. Errors: Process cleanup was not verified

Quit Anyway ends Sphyrna without confirming that every session process stopped. Processes Sphyrna could not stop may keep running.

Three ways on, stacked as macOS stacks three. Try Again is the default. Quit Anyway is the destructive one, in the failure text, and never reads as a success.

The session list is damaged

Sphyrna could not open the session list

The file that holds the session list is damaged. Sphyrna keeps the damaged file next to it until a session is permanently deleted. Restore Backup brings back the list as of 20 Oct 2026 at 09:12; sessions added after that are missing from it. Start Fresh begins with an empty list instead. Your Claude transcripts are not touched.

At launch, before any window. Without a backup the alert says so and offers Start Fresh and Quit. A catalog newer than this build stops at Home's own message instead: "Sphyrna could not open its catalog", the error, and "The catalog file was left untouched. Nothing was created or overwritten."

05

What to react to

The content is the edge and system states as built (UI-MAP), named Sphyrna. This page draws them in Ink and asks where it changes them. A yes, a no, or "more like X" on each is enough.

  1. The first launch's school. A hammerhead, the whale shark and a few small ones roaming behind the message. Remoras that keep to the big two, or threshers on their own? Without Claude, the triangle takes the icon's place, with no shark.
  2. Home's hammerhead. One, always circling the band on a fixed loop of 180 seconds, with no thresher. Slow and quiet enough?
  3. The art under Reduce Motion. It moves anyway, as the working spinner does, and Settings → Theme → Animated art stops it. On by default, right?
  4. The sidebar's empty lines. "No projects yet." only, with Home holding the explanation and the button, and "No sessions" under an empty project. OK?
  5. Nothing needs you. The band's sentence says it, and the Needs you card stays empty with the line that says what appears there. Clearer than today's card?
  6. The ended terminal and the record. One flush line, Show Details then Resume, Resume filled but not on Return. The record says the state and Resume first, and the facts after. Anything to change?
  7. One banner family. Phase 2's box under the header too, one at a time with "+N", by weight, each tone with its own symbol. Or keep today's rows, all shown at once?
  8. The menu bar menu and the Dock. Needs you, Working, then the commands, listing exactly what the Dock counts. And a banner only for approvals, questions and plans, as built. Right?
  9. The alerts. Quit keeps Cancel as its default, so Cancel is the filled one; the stacked three-button alerts follow macOS. OK?
