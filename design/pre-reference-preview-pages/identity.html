Sphyrna

The app takes the hammerhead's name: Sphyrna is its genus, from the Greek for hammer. The icon is the hammerhead the app already draws, facing left on black. Home's assistant becomes Remora, the fish that rides with sharks.

01

One name

Sphyrna is the hammerhead's genus, from the Greek sphyra, a hammer. One word, no pun, written the same in every language.

02

The icon is the art

The app's own ASCII engine draws the icon: the same field, ramp and eyes as Home's band and the new-session page, held still. Rising, your pick: the hammerhead climbing to the left on black.

03

A mark for the menu bar

The hammerhead seen from the front, your pick: the hammer with an eye at each tip, the dorsal fin, the jaw and the pectoral fins, in one colour on a 20 pt canvas.

04

Remora arrives on Home

Lombot becomes Remora. When Home opens with a new brief, the hammerhead brings the remora in, lets it go at the start of the brief and swims on. The remora stays there, beside its sentence.

05

One drawing everywhere

First launch, About, the alerts, the Dock and the banners show the same hammerhead. The art keeps its rules: it never swims behind something you read.

06

What changes

The name wherever you read it, with 1.5.0. The code's names (LoomCore and the rest) stay. The bundle id and the data folder are a decision of their own.

03

The menu bar mark

Front, your pick: the hammerhead seen from the front, in one colour on a 20 pt canvas, 18 wide, like today's mark. macOS tints it white or black with the bar, and a status badge cuts into its lower right corner. The table shows it at real size in every state.

Front your pick

The hammer with an eye at each tip, the dorsal fin above, the jaw and the pectoral fins below. The eyes are holes in the hammer, so the bar shows through them.

  1. Canvas20 by 20 pt, the mark 18 wide, as LoomTheme.MenuBar draws today's. The paths use only M, L, C and Z, the commands the app's own path reader takes.
  2. BadgeA 7 pt dot at the lower right, cut 5.2 around: needs you, finished or failed. Working draws the braille spinner in the same slot (§4).
  3. ColourIdle, it is a template image and macOS tints it. With a badge the app draws it: white on a dark bar, black on a light one.
  4. Not ASCIIAt 16 pt a character cell is one or two pixels wide, so the menu bar gets a drawn mark; the art's characters start at the icon.
04

Remora on Home

Lombot's new name, and its place on Home. When Home opens with a new brief, the hammerhead swims in with the remora on its flank and lets it go at the start of the brief. The remora turns to its sentence, swims in place while Remora reads, and holds still once the brief is written. It stays there while Home is open, so Remora has a face on Home. The frame plays when it scrolls into view; Replay plays it again, and a frame of the storyboard holds the window at its moment. This page plays the motion even when macOS asks to reduce it; the top bar's Reduce Motion switch shows the reduced version.

05

What the rename touches

The name changes with 1.5.0, as D-68 ships it; the 0.4.4 you use every day stays Loom until then. Three things are yours to decide, because macOS treats them as the app's identity.

With 1.5.0 the words and the art

  • The app's name in the bundle, so the menus, About, the Dock, Finder and the banners say Sphyrna.
  • Every sentence that names the app: "Sphyrna could not find Claude", "Quit Sphyrna and stop 2 working sessions?", Settings' help lines.
  • Lombot becomes Remora in Home, in Settings' tab and in the vault note Remora.md.
  • The icon, the menu bar mark and About's art; README and its renders.
  • Release files: Sphyrna-1.5.0.dmg; the development build becomes Sphyrna Dev.

Yours to decide macOS's identity

  • The bundle id local.loom.desktop. A new id is a new app to macOS: notifications and Open at login are asked for again.
  • The data folder ~/Library/Application Support/Loom: moved once at first launch, or kept under the old name.
  • The repository gencberke/loom: renamed (GitHub forwards the old address) or kept.

Stays what nobody reads

  • The code's names: LoomCore, LoomCatalogUI, LoomTheme and the rest.
  • The planner's tree loom-dev and the decision log's history.
  • Claude's own files in ~/.claude; the sessions are Claude's, not the app's.
06

What to react to

Round 2 carries your answers: Rising, whiter, smaller and centred; Front in the menu bar; Remora's arrival on Home. A yes, a no or "more like X" on each is enough. Once you accept, the name, the icon and the mark go into the design documents as a decision, and the icon is made for the build.

  1. The icon. Is round 2 the one: whiter, an eighth smaller, centred?
  2. Remora's arrival. The pair swims in, the remora lets go at the start of the brief and turns to its sentence, the hammerhead swims on. Right length, right place?
  3. When it plays. Only when Home opens with a new brief (the first Home of the day, and after Catch me up), or every time you open Home?
  4. Reduce Motion. It is on on your Mac, so by the art's rule (§17) you would get the fade, not the arrival. Keep the rule, or let the arrival play anyway, as the working spinner does (D-12)?
  5. The name in type. Still open from round 1: SF Pro, as macOS writes app names, or the mono lockup for README and the disk image?
  6. macOS's identity. Still open: change the bundle id and move the data folder with 1.5.0, or keep both under the new name? And the repository: rename it or keep it?
