Loom feedback round 3: session header and Usage page

13 Oct 2026, after 0.4.1. Static mock-ups drawn at real size (1 CSS pixel = 1 pt) with the theme's values; sample data, not screenshots. Chosen on 13 Oct (D-53): the header is A with the branch, and the Usage page is U1. The third item of the round (subagent rows that look finished too early) is a bug and is handled separately.

1 · The session header

The row above the tabs keeps all it holds today: the provider mark, the session title, its state, the project, Stop and ⋯. What changes is the order and the shapes. Today the state and the project trail the title, so they move whenever the title is longer or shorter, and Stop (a tinted capsule) and ⋯ (a bare glyph) look like two different families. Every option keeps the row 36 pt high, so the terminal does not lose a line.

B · A title block, then the actions

The title and everything that describes it form one block of two lines. The first line is the title. The second line holds the state, the project and the branch. The branch is new to the header; it is optional and already shows on the card. The actions are one group on the right. Both lines fit the same 36 pt row. Nothing moves when the state changes, because the state is always the first word of the second line.

Planner: B. It gives the row a clear structure: one block says what the session is and how it stands, and one group holds what you can do. It fits the same 36 pt row, and the state is always the first word of the second line, so nothing moves when it changes. A is the conservative choice: one line, fixed places, nothing new. C puts the state first, but its pill changes width with the state, so the title moves.

2 · The Usage page

Everything the page shows today stays: the period's total and estimate, the plan limits, the token breakdown, the bars, the local five hours and the models. The changes are containers, order and weight. Today every block sits loose on the window background, the plan limits sit in the middle, and two different "5 hours" are easy to mix up: the plan's limit window and Loom's local activity cells. The five totals also have equal weight, although cache reads dominate. Sample data: 7 days, three models.

U2: the three numbers most often looked for are tiles at the top: the period's total and the two plan windows. The details follow in cards. The tokens and the models sit side by side; in this narrower model table the share moves into the tooltip.

Planner: U1. It separates the subjects most clearly and keeps every detail in its full form, including the model shares. Its order puts the plan limits first. U2 is quicker at a glance, but its side-by-side cards leave the model table without its share column at the Settings window's 680 pt width.
