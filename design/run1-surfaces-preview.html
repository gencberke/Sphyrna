1. Sidebar footer bar inside the sidebar, 38 pt, + left, usage chip middle, gear right

The bar replaces today's two-button footer and keeps its height (38 pt) and side padding (12 pt). The chip is one 24 pt button, radius 6, 8 pt padding, SF Pro 11 medium with tabular digits. Click opens Settings at Usage. Variant (a) is text only. Variant (b) puts a five-bar activity meter (last five hours, hourly bins, current hour in primary text) before today's tokens. Hover wants the Mac's own pointer, so it is drawn as a separate state.

2. Settings window dark, 680 x 520, opens at Usage from the footer chip

Navigation: a top section switcher (General · Usage · About), centered on the 26 pt title-bar centerline. With three sections a left list would spend 160 pt of a 680 pt window on three words and read as the left rail that D-34 rejects. The switcher reuses the selected-card look (white 7 % fill, 10 % stroke, radius 6) on a 6 % track. The window is forced dark, opaque panel colour, no glass, and keeps one size for all three sections so it never jumps. The window title is "Settings" (hidden, the switcher takes its place).

3. Conversation tab strip inside the detail panel, between the header row and the terminal

The strip is 32 pt: tabs are 24 pt high (the chrome-button height) with 4 pt vertical and 8 pt side padding, 4 pt between tabs, radius 6. A tab is [12 pt status indicator] 6 pt [title, SF Pro 12 medium, truncating], padding 8 each side, width 112 to 200 pt. The selected tab also reserves a 16 pt close slot (4 pt trailing padding); on other tabs the close button appears on hover as a 16 pt square over the end of the title, so titles keep the room. It is not drawn with fewer than two tabs and the panel then looks exactly as today. There is no "+" button: new conversations start from the footer and the project menus; "Open in Tab" pins the selection. Tabs sit on the panel colour with no divider, so the strip reads as part of the header, not a second bar.

4. Rationale and open choices

Taken from the references

  • T3 usage page: period control top right (24 h, 7 d, 30 d, 90 d), a big headline number at top left with a labelled estimate under it, a totals row (input, output, cache write, cache read, cache share), a daily chart, and a per-model table with tokens, share and cost columns and a thin share bar.
  • Layout guidance: tabs above the main content (surface 3), the bottom bar becomes the compact footer inside the sidebar (surface 1).
  • Identity A: every colour is an A token (panel, text tiers, white-alpha fills, hairline 10 %, selected look 7 % + 10 % stroke); radii 6/8/10; weight before size; sentence case, no exclamation marks.

Left out on purpose

  • Cost as the headline, cost line and cost-by-type bars: tokens are the headline, cost is one secondary line labelled an estimate, and unknown models show "—".
  • The filled area chart, orange series colour and glow: bars are neutral and quiet; the single accent stays for focus rings and default buttons.
  • Plan limits (5-hour, weekly, the "Limits" toggle): not reachable locally (DP-U4). A labelled, approximate local 5-hour activity block stands in.
  • Environment and provider split, speed tiers, "Cost / Tokens" toggle: Claude only for now. The table's first column is model name; a provider mark can be added left of it for Codex without changing the layout.
  • Left rail, right sidebar, a "+" tab button, a second header bar, session counts per model.

Lightness

  • No images, charts or custom drawing: bars are rounded rectangles, the activity block is five cells, the meter is five bars. All are static; nothing animates, no timers.
  • The chip is one text line; the Settings body is a plain scroll view of text and rectangles, built only when the window is open.
  • The chase appears only in conversation status, so "indexing" never reads as "a conversation is working".

Open choices and recommendation

(b) is the better glance at "am I busy now" but the meter is unlabeled shape language and the 5-hour figure is approximate; it can be added later in the same slot.
