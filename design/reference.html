sphyrna · ink · the design reference

Ink

Sphyrna's design language, and every surface it draws, as decided. One calm black canvas where many agents work, for one power user: competent and quiet, with a school of sharks for character and plain words for everything else.

01

One canvas

The window is one surface. Hairlines divide it, and nothing is boxed twice. Only what you act on is lifted: the composer, a menu, a card.

02

Two voices

SF Pro for what you read. SF Mono for what the machine says: branches, paths, times, tokens, counts and keys.

03

Colour means state

The chrome is grey. Periwinkle marks focus and the default action. Orange, blue and red are status only. A project's tone is its mark, the tint of its tabs and the tint of its selected session.

04

One control family

One height, radius, hover and focus ring for every control. Session tabs are pills and view tabs are underlined, wherever they appear.

05

Two reading points

The sidebar tells how every session is doing. The header tells how this one is doing and what you can do. Everything else is detail.

06

A school of sharks

A hammerhead, a thresher, a whale shark and the remora, drawn in ASCII. They swim on empty canvases and while something waits, never behind the terminal, a list or text being read.
